Dropforge Survivor — Account & Data Deletion

Published by Cherryware for the game Dropforge Survivor (com.cherryware.dropforge).

This page explains how to request deletion of your Dropforge Survivor account and the data associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to dropforgesurvivor@gmail.com from any address you can access.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my Dropforge Survivor account.
  3. In the email, include your in-game Player ID (shown in the game under Settings / Profile) so we can locate your account. If you cannot find it, describe your account (approximate creation date, character name) and we will help identify it.

We confirm every request by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

DataResult
Player profile and account recordPermanently deleted
Game progress (levels, currencies, items, achievements, quests)Permanently deleted
Device and advertising identifiers linked to the accountPermanently deleted
Auction house listings and trade history tied to the accountPermanently deleted
Analytics and attribution eventsDeleted or irreversibly anonymised

What may be retained

  • Purchase and transaction records — kept for up to 5 years where required by tax and accounting law. These records are not linked to your game profile after deletion.
  • Aggregated, non-identifying statistics — retained indefinitely; these contain no information that can identify you.

Deleting data without deleting your account

We do not currently offer partial data deletion. To remove your data, use the account deletion process above.
